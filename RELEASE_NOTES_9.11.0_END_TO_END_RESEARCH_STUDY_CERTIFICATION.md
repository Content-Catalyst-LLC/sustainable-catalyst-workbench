# Workbench v9.11.0 — End-to-End Research Study Certification

v9.11.0 adds a structural certification layer over the complete Workbench v9 research lifecycle. It evaluates whether researcher-selected study objects resolve, retain content-addressed integrity, form coherent cross-stage lineage, and satisfy an explicit certification profile.

Certification dimensions include profile completeness, object integrity, lineage coherence, reproducibility readiness, portability readiness, and platform-wide handoff readiness. Missing stages, unresolved objects, and relationship mismatches remain explicit gaps rather than being silently repaired.

Certification is **not** scientific validity, peer review, hypothesis acceptance, publication approval, or a governed Platform Core claim. Workbench records a reproducibility/traceability certification envelope and can prepare a Core binding plan only; dispatch remains explicit.
