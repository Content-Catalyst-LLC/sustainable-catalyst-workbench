# Sustainable Catalyst Workbench v5.3.0

**Computational Blackboard, Creative Mathematics & Physical Prototyping**

Adds a deterministic symbolic Blackboard, music/acoustics mathematics, creative mathematical forms, Arduino/ESP32/Raspberry Pi/PYNQ/Verilog/VHDL prototype scaffolds, a distinct homepage computational-instrument widget, and a darker advanced Graph Mathematics presentation with process presets.

New shortcodes:

- `[sc_workbench_blackboard]`
- `[sc_workbench_music_mathematics]`
- `[sc_workbench_creative_mathematics]`
- `[sc_workbench_prototype_bench]`
- `[sc_workbench_homepage_instrument]`
- `[sc_workbench_v530_showcase]`

The studio catalog grows from 30 to 34 entries. v5.1 CAS and v5.2 graph routes remain additive and backward compatible.
