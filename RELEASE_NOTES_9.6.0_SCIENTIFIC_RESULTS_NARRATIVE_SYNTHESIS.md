# Workbench v9.6.0 — Scientific Results & Narrative Synthesis

Adds a reproducible synthesis layer over statistical analyses, uncertainty/sensitivity studies, model calibrations, analysis-board snapshots, and publication/evidence packages.

## Capabilities
- Researcher-authored abstract, methods, results, interpretation, limitations, conclusion, and narrative statements.
- Statement-to-source traceability with unresolved references surfaced explicitly.
- Evidence-reference preservation without converting references into governed claims.
- Content-addressed immutable synthesis records.
- Publication and Platform Core handoff planning without automatic dispatch.

## Boundaries
Workbench does not invent findings, accept hypotheses, decide statistical significance, infer causality, resolve contradictions, publish automatically, or replace Platform Core governance.
