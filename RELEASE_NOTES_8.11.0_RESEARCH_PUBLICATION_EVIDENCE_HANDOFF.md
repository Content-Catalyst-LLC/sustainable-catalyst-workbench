# Workbench v8.11.0 — Research Publication & Evidence Handoff

Workbench v8.11.0 turns immutable v8.10 analysis-board snapshots into governed, content-addressed publication and evidence handoff packages. It preserves researcher-authored narrative, source hashes, figures, comparison provenance, and explicit evidence references while generating non-dispatching plans for Platform Core, Knowledge Library, Research Lab, and external publication workflows.

## Highlights
- Immutable v8.10 analysis snapshot required as the publication handoff anchor.
- Publication metadata assembly without rewriting researcher methods, assumptions, findings, or notes.
- Evidence manifest with resolved and unresolved evidence references made explicit.
- Figure, comparison, execution, asset, and source-hash provenance preserved.
- Content-addressed immutable handoff packages with integrity validation.
- Explicit destination plans for Platform Core, Knowledge Library, Research Lab, and external publication export.
- Two-phase Platform Core binding plan; no automatic dispatch or persistence.

## Boundaries
The handoff is not a publication decision or scientific-validity assessment. It does not generate findings, infer causality or significance, select preferred models, resolve evidence conflicts, mutate source objects, or publish/dispatch automatically.
